What stands out

  • Review counts in the pack ran from 2 to 261. The fewest belonged to Business A, at #1. In at least one search a listing with fewer reviews sat above one with more, so review count alone did not set the order.
  • 7 businesses for 9 spots. The most frequent listing, Business A, held 2 of them. Every search returned a full three-business pack, so getting in means displacing a listing that is already there, not filling an empty seat.
  • Across the region, for context (trade-level, not Surrey-specific): in 27 of 52 landscaping searches across 18 Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley cities in the same sweep (2026-09-09), the #1 map result had fewer than 50 reviews.

What that means for a landscaping company in Surrey

Review count is the most visible thing in a map pack, and it is one a business can grow on purpose. It is not the only thing Google weighs: distance from the person searching, the business category on the Google profile, and how closely the profile and website match the search all matter too. That is why a listing with fewer reviews can sit above one with more.
