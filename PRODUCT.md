# PRODUCT.md — rankwise.ca

## Register
brand — marketing/landing surface; design IS the product. (The operator/client portal lives elsewhere.)

## What this is
The public site for Rankwise: managed local-search (Google Maps / Map Pack) marketing for local service businesses — 11 categories (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, auto repair, painting, moving, cleaning, pest control, garage doors), Metro Vancouver + Fraser Valley. Sole-proprietor founder + heavy automation. Canonical business truth: `../rankwise-dashboard/vault/POSITIONING.md` — link, don't restate.

## Target users
Owners of local service businesses ($150k–$4M/yr revenue, solo to ~15 staff). Busy, skeptical, burned-by-agencies. They decide from proof and plain English, not marketing language. They read on phones between jobs.

## Purpose / desired outcome
One outcome: a booked 15-minute free audit call (Cal.com embed). Everything on the page exists to earn that click with evidence — live market studies, honest pricing, and the written billing-pause guarantee.

## Offer (fixed — never invent numbers)
Three set tiers: Starter $450 · Foundation $750 · Growth $1,250 CAD, month-to-month, no negotiation. Guarantee: billing-pause mechanic only (30-day optimization-done on Starter; 90-day Map-Pack milestone on Foundation/Growth). BANNED wording on every surface: "no payment", "you don't pay", "money back", "guaranteed rankings", "free month" + semantic equivalents (canonical list: seller-kit 03-must-not-say.md).

## Brand personality
Three words: **documented, unhurried, accountable.** The committed aesthetic lane (shipping, identity-preservation wins): a "filed report" — cream paper (#F6F5F0), ink borders (#1B1C18), single hard offset shadows, Cabinet Grotesk display, monospace filing labels used sparingly, forest green (#16352A) + amber (#C68A3A) accents, numbered section labels ("08 · …") as the site's deliberate filing system. Evidence-first: real data tables, real maps, real terms — never decoration pretending to be data.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS gradient-hero landing pages; glassmorphism; hero-metric stat rows.
- Agency-hype voice ("10x your leads!!"); any unverifiable claim; fake scarcity/timers.
- Cream-and-serif "editorial magazine" drift — this is a filed report, not a magazine.

## Strategic design principles
1. Proof before promise — show the data element before the claim it supports.
2. Plain English beats jargon; a busy owner must parse any section in one pass on a phone.
3. The guarantee is the differentiator — it appears in words, never paraphrased into banned forms.
4. One CTA (book the audit); never compete with it.
5. Wide artifacts (tables, maps) scroll inside their own container — the page never scrolls sideways.
