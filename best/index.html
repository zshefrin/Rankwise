This is your business — and it's ranked here whether you claim it or not.

Claiming lets you keep the details shown here right: your hours, services, a short description, your photos, and public responses to what customers say. That's the version people see when they look you up here, and the version the AI assistants that read this page pull from — so it should be the one you'd put your name on.

What claiming never does is change your rank. Scores come only from public signals and aren't for sale — not to you, not to anyone, not to our own clients. A claimed listing shows as 'Owner-confirmed,' and the number stays exactly what the public data says.
