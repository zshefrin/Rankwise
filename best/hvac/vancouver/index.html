How this ranking works (summary)

Every business is scored out of 100 from public signals only — methodology v2.1.0, published in full at rankwise.ca/best/methodology/:

Scroll to see the full table →

ComponentWeightWhat it measures
review volume28 pointsHow many Google reviews the business has — a larger body of independent customer reviews is stronger evidence, counted on a curve so the step from 10 to 100 reviews matters more than the step from 1,000 to 1,100.
rating quality28 pointsThe business's Google star rating, weighted by how many reviews stand behind it — a 4.8 backed by 900 reviews outscores a 5.0 backed by 6.
map pack presence23 pointsHow often the business appears in Google's own top-3 map results across the tracked searches for this trade and city, with a higher position earning more credit.
web presence15 pointsWhether the business has a working website, serves it over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and publishes structured data that search and AI engines can verify — full credit when that data identifies it as a local service business, partial credit for other structured data, and no credit when none is found.
citation consistency6 pointsWhether the phone number and address published on the business's own website match what's shown on its Google listing — checked for a limited number of businesses per refresh cycle within a fixed API budget; a business not yet checked this cycle earns no points for this component, never a penalty.

Scores render only from captured public data; where a signal was not captured, the card says so — nothing is estimated or invented. Businesses cannot pay to be listed, scored, or moved. Inclusion: the pool is the businesses Google surfaces for this city's searches — serving the city, not necessarily headquartered in it (full inclusion criteria in the methodology).
